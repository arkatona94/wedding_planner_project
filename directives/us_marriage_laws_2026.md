# 2026 US Marriage Legality Database

This document contains a breakdown of marriage license requirements across all 50 states and Washington, D.C. 

> **Important Disclaimer:** Marriage laws are subject to local legislative changes. Users should always confirm specific document requirements and fees with the County Clerk or Probate Court in the jurisdiction where the ceremony will occur.
